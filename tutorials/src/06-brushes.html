画笔与橡皮擦

Brushes and the eraser

画笔在当前图层上放上颜色或纹理；橡皮擦减去或露出下层。两者都要先确认：你在哪一层？硬度、不透明度和流量各是多少？画在错误的图层上是最常见的挫败来源。

A brush puts color or texture on the current layer; the eraser removes it or reveals what is underneath. Always check: which layer is active, and what are hardness, opacity, and flow? Painting on the wrong layer is the most common frustration.

在 SimplePhoto 中

In SimplePhoto

  • 画笔工具（B）：常规绘制。同组还有铅笔（更硬的像素边缘）、混合器画笔、颜色替换。
  • 橡皮擦（E）：同组有背景橡皮擦和魔术橡皮擦，后两者按颜色或背景逻辑清除，使用前先在副本或单独图层上试。
  • 窗口 → 画笔设置（F5）和 画笔 面板：改大小、硬度、形状动态等。
  • 许多键盘习惯与 Photoshop 相近：用 [ / ] 改变大小（若当前快捷键方案保留了这些绑定）。
  • Brush Tool (B) is the general painter. The same group includes Pencil (harder pixel edges), Mixer Brush, and Color Replacement.
  • Eraser (E), plus Background Eraser and Magic Eraser, which clear by color or background logic — try them on a copy or a spare layer first.
  • Window → Brush Settings (F5) and the Brushes panel change size, hardness, and shape dynamics.
  • Many keyboard habits match Photoshop, including [ / ] for size when those bindings are still in your shortcut set.

控制笔触

Controlling a stroke

硬度高适合明确边缘，硬度低适合过渡。不透明度决定每一笔最多盖住多少；流量则影响颜料「流出」的快慢，二者叠加时要慢慢试。需要润饰照片时，常常在低不透明度下多次轻涂，而不是一笔盖死。

High hardness suits a crisp edge; low hardness suits a transition. Opacity caps how much a stroke can cover; flow controls how quickly “paint” is laid down — try them together slowly. For photo retouching, many light, low-opacity strokes usually beat one heavy pass.

仅 Photoshop 的概念

Photoshop-only notes

Photoshop 的云端笔刷同步、部分毛发级 Super Resolution 笔刷市场，以及和 Adobe Fresco 互传的笔刷预设，不属于 SimplePhoto。请使用本机画笔预设与画笔设置面板。

Photoshop’s cloud-synced brushes, some marketplace packs, and Fresco brush sharing are not part of SimplePhoto. Use local brush presets and the Brush Settings panel.
