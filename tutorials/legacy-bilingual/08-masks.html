图层蒙版

Layer masks

蒙版用灰度图控制图层的可见范围：白色显示，黑色隐藏，灰色半透明。它不是橡皮擦——像素还在，以后仍可把隐藏的部分画回来。这是从「会修图」走向「改得起」的关键一步。

A mask is a grayscale map of a layer’s visibility: white reveals, black hides, gray is partial. It is not an eraser — the pixels remain, so you can paint hidden areas back later. This is the step from “I can retouch” to “I can still change my mind.”

在 SimplePhoto 中

In SimplePhoto

在图层面板为当前层 添加图层蒙版（也可在 图层 菜单中选择显示全部、隐藏全部，或根据选区显示/隐藏）。有选区时，添加蒙版常会直接按选区生成。用画笔在蒙版上涂黑或涂白来修改；点图层缩略图是改像素，点蒙版缩略图才是改蒙版。也可以添加矢量蒙版，用路径得到更硬、可再编辑的边缘。

Add a layer mask from the Layers panel or the Layer menu (reveal all, hide all, or reveal/hide based on a selection). If a selection is active, the mask often follows it immediately. Paint black or white on the mask to edit it; click the layer thumbnail to edit pixels, and the mask thumbnail to edit the mask. Vector masks are also available when you want a hard, path-based edge you can still revise.

常见用法

Typical uses

  • 把抠出的人物放到新背景上，用蒙版修发丝，而不是把背景像素删掉。
  • 让调整图层只作用于天空或皮肤：给调整图层加蒙版，把不想改的部分涂黑。
  • 做渐变过渡（例如上下曝光不同），在蒙版上拉渐变，比直接涂抹像素更容易改。
  • Place a cut-out person on a new background and refine hair on the mask, instead of deleting background pixels.
  • Limit an adjustment layer to a sky or to skin: add a mask and paint out the areas you do not want changed.
  • Build a gradient transition (different exposure top and bottom) on the mask; it is easier to revise than painting the pixels themselves.

检查蒙版时，可以对照边缘是否漏光或发虚。错了就换画笔硬度，或回到「选择并遮住」再输出一次蒙版，不必从头重来整张图。

When you inspect a mask, look for leaks or mushy edges. If something is off, change brush hardness or run Select and Mask again and output a new mask — you do not have to rebuild the whole document.

仅 Photoshop 的概念

Photoshop-only notes

部分「选择主体并自动生成蒙版」的云端按钮、以及生成式填充工作区里的任务蒙版，是 Photoshop 产品流程。SimplePhoto 用本地选区工具和「选择并遮住」来生成蒙版。

Some cloud “select subject and auto-mask” buttons, and task masks inside the Generative Fill workspace, belong to Photoshop’s product flow. SimplePhoto builds masks with local selection tools and Select and Mask.
