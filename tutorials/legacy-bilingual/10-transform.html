第 10 章 · 变形

Chapter 10 · Transform

变换

Transform

变换用来缩放、旋转、倾斜或透视移动图层或选中的像素。合成一张新图时，几乎总会把素材放到合适的大小和角度。注意：对普通栅格图层反复大幅变换，画质会一次次变差；智能对象可以减轻这个问题（第 14 章）。

Transform scales, rotates, skews, or applies perspective to a layer or to selected pixels. Almost every composite needs a piece moved to the right size and angle. Beware: repeating large transforms on an ordinary raster layer softens it each time. Smart Objects reduce that problem (chapter 14).

在 SimplePhoto 中

In SimplePhoto

编辑 → 自由变换（⌘T / Ctrl+T）是最常用入口：拖角缩放，在框外旋转，确认后提交。编辑 → 变换 子菜单还提供缩放、旋转、斜切、扭曲、透视和变形。需要先复制再变换时，可使用「自由变换副本」（⌘⌥T / Ctrl+Alt+T）。对智能对象还可以使用智能对象变形。

Edit → Free Transform (⌘T / Ctrl+T) is the usual entry: drag a corner to scale, drag outside the box to rotate, and confirm to commit. Edit → Transform also offers Scale, Rotate, Skew, Distort, Perspective, and Warp. Free Transform a Copy (⌘⌥T / Ctrl+Alt+T) transforms a duplicate. Smart Objects can use Smart Object Warp as well.

操作建议

Working tips

按住 Shift 是否锁定比例，取决于当前工具选项和偏好设置，变换前先看提示或选项栏，不要想当然。放大超过原图像素时会出现发虚，能换更高分辨率的素材就换。变形和透视适合贴合墙面或包装盒，但要对照消失点，避免「看起来几乎对、其实在漂」。

Whether Shift constrains proportions depends on the current options and preferences — check the options bar instead of assuming. Scaling far beyond the source resolution looks soft; use a higher-resolution source when you can. Warp and Perspective help a graphic sit on a wall or a box, but watch vanishing points so it does not merely “look almost right.”

仅 Photoshop 的概念

Photoshop-only notes

有些生成式「扩展并补全变换后的空白」功能属于 Photoshop。SimplePhoto 的变换不会自动用 AI 填补空缺，空白处要自己用其他图层或仿制来补。

Some generative “fill the empty area after a transform” features are Photoshop-only. SimplePhoto will not invent pixels with Firefly after a transform; cover gaps yourself with another layer or cloning.
