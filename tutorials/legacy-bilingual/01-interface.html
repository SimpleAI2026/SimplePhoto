Chapter 01 · Basics

界面与画布

Interface and the canvas

图像编辑软件的界面可以想成一张工作台：中间是画布，四周是工具和面板。先认识这些区域，后面每一章才知道该看哪里。

Think of the editor as a workbench: the canvas sits in the middle, with tools and panels around it. Learn these regions first so later chapters have a place to point to.

你会看到什么

What you will see

  • 顶栏：文档名、菜单、工作区切换、外观和语言。
  • 工具栏：选择、裁剪、画笔、文字、修复、形状、抓手和缩放等。同类工具往往共用一个快捷键，长按或右键可展开隐藏项。
  • 画布：正在编辑的图像。使用抓手工具或按住空格可以平移；Z 缩放。
  • 面板：图层、历史记录、画笔设置、属性等。菜单 窗口 可显示或隐藏它们。
  • Title bar: document name, menus, workspace switcher, appearance, and language.
  • Toolbar: select, crop, brush, type, retouch, shapes, hand, and zoom. Related tools often share a shortcut; press and hold or right-click to expand the group.
  • Canvas: the image you are editing. Use the Hand tool or hold Space to pan; Z zooms.
  • Panels: Layers, History, Brush Settings, Properties, and more. The Window menu shows or hides them.

在 SimplePhoto 中

In SimplePhoto

起始主页完成后进入编辑视图。按 V 使用移动工具，H 抓手，Z 缩放，R 旋转视图（改变观察角度，不一定旋转像素）。视图 菜单提供适应屏幕、实际像素等。历史记录在 窗口 → 历史记录。图层面板默认可用 F7 开关。

After Home, you enter the editor. V is Move, H Hand, Z Zoom, and R Rotate View (this turns your view; it does not always rotate pixels). The View menu has Fit on Screen and actual-pixel zoom. History lives under Window → History. Toggle Layers with F7.

画布与文档

Canvas versus document

「画布」是文档的工作范围，像素存在于此。图像可以比窗口小，也可以大到需要平移。先习惯「我在哪一层、当前放大到多少」，比急着修图更重要。需要撤销时，用 编辑 → 还原 或历史记录面板逐步后退。

The canvas is the document’s working area — the pixels live here. The image may be smaller than the window or large enough to pan. Getting used to “which layer am I on, and at what zoom?” matters more than rushing into retouching. Undo via Edit or step back in the History panel.

仅 Photoshop 的概念

Photoshop-only notes

Photoshop 还有「发现」面板、工作区云同步，以及部分依赖 Adobe 云的上下文任务栏功能。SimplePhoto 也有工作区和上下文任务栏（窗口 → Contextual Task Bar），但不会同步到 Adobe 云。

Photoshop also has a Discover panel, cloud-synced workspaces, and cloud-backed Contextual Task Bar features. SimplePhoto has workspaces and a Contextual Task Bar (Window → Contextual Task Bar), but nothing syncs to Adobe’s cloud.
